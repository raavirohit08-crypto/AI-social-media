# GreenLeaf Coffee Roasters: Brand Guide

## Who we are
GreenLeaf Coffee Roasters is a small-batch specialty coffee roastery and café in Visakhapatnam.
We roast beans from small farms in the Araku Valley and Coorg and sell whole beans, ground coffee,
cold brew bottles and brewing equipment online and in our café.

## Brand voice
- Warm, friendly and a little playful, like a barista who remembers your order.
- Simple everyday words. No jargon, no snobbery about coffee.
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- We celebrate farmers and the story behind every cup.
- Emojis: yes, but sparingly (☕🌱 are our favourites).

## Words and topics to avoid
- Never say "cheap", "best in the world" or "guaranteed".
- No health claims (we do not say coffee cures or prevents anything).
- No politics or religion. No negative comments about other brands.

## Target audience
Young professionals and students aged 20-35, home-brewing beginners, and gift buyers.

## Hashtags we use
#GreenLeafCoffee #SmallBatchCoffee #ArakuCoffee #CoffeeLovers #ColdBrew

## Call-to-action style
Soft and inviting: "Come say hi", "Tell us how you brew", "Order your first bag".
