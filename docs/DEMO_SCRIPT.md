# Demo script (3-4 minutes)

Goal from the reviewer: show that **the same topic produces three distinct versions for Instagram, LinkedIn and X
while keeping the supplied brand facts.**

1. **Brand & Knowledge** - show the saved brand profile and the sample documents (`Knowledge/`). Click *Build / refresh*.
2. **Generate Posts** - topic: `Launching our new Salted Caramel Cold Brew this Friday`
   - platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter) · versions per platform: **3** · "Use brand knowledge" ticked.
   - Point out: live streaming preview; 3 distinct versions per platform; the price **Rs 170** and "Friday" taken from the
     brand documents; the X posts are under 280 characters; "Brand facts came from: ..." line.
3. **Keeping knowledge current** - on *Brand & Knowledge* delete or add a document, go back to *Generate Posts* and show that
   the new facts are used immediately (no manual refresh needed).
4. **Improve Post** - paste a draft, choose *Make it shorter*; show it still follows the brand voice and avoids "cheap / guaranteed".
5. **Content Calendar** - request 14 days at 3 posts per week; show exactly 6 rows, no duplicates, max 2 per day.
6. **Evaluation** - open `evaluation/results/eval_*.md` and show the checks and the tone scores.

Record the screen (e.g. Windows Game Bar `Win + G`, or OBS), upload to Google Drive / YouTube (unlisted) and paste the
link under "Demo recording" at the top of `README.md`.
